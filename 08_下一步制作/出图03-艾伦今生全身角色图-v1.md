# 出图 03：艾伦今生全身角色图 v1

## 目标

锁定今生主角艾伦的视觉。  
他现在还不是满级圣骑士，而是一个从槐安村出发的 18 岁少年；但他的身体里已经藏着前世的战斗惯性。

## 提示词

```text
anime cinematic fantasy, emotionally grounded character design, clean high-quality production art, consistent character identity, elegant but grounded, soft painterly detail, no text, no logo, no watermark.

Full body character reference of Allen, an 18-year-old young man from a quiet fantasy village. He has dark brown medium-length hair, slightly messy but natural, warm amber-brown eyes, a youthful face with a trace of old sadness he does not understand, and a very faint single white streak beginning at his left temple, subtle enough to feel like a hidden omen rather than a strong design feature.

He wears simple travel clothes from a rural village: off-white linen shirt, dark brown practical vest, worn leather belt, dark practical trousers, soft leather boots, and a plain short cloak suitable for walking long roads. His outfit should look handmade and lived-in, not noble and not heroic armor.

On his left palm is a subtle warm golden seal mark that glows faintly under the skin. He carries a simple one-handed sword made by a village blacksmith, not ornate, with a half-sun or half-moon mark on the hilt. The sword should look reliable but ordinary, not legendary yet.

Pose: relaxed but with an unconscious combat-ready balance. His feet and shoulders naturally settle into a stance slightly too trained for a village boy, as if his body remembers something his mind does not. His expression is calm, curious, and quietly unsettled.

Light neutral background, full body from head to toe, front view with slight three-quarter angle, high quality anime fantasy character concept art.

Avoid: full knight armor, royal clothing, modern outfit, oversized weapon, aggressive hero pose, old man face, large white hair, glowing superpower overload, text, logo, watermark.
```

## 验收标准

- 他应该像能从槐安村出发的人，不像王都贵族。
- 他有主角感，但不是满级英雄感。
- 左手金印能看清，但不能抢戏。
- 左鬓白发要非常轻，只是伏笔。
- 站姿要有一点“不自觉的战斗重心”。
- 和前世艾兰有呼应，但不能穿银甲。
