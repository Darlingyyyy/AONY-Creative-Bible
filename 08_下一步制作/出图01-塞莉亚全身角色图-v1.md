# 出图 01：塞莉亚全身角色图 v1

## 目标

锁定 AONY 的女主气质。她不是普通圣女，不是战斗型女主，不是可爱卖萌角色。她是一个“会发光但随时会消失的少女”。

## 提示词

```text
anime cinematic fantasy, emotionally grounded character design, clean high-quality production art, consistent character identity, elegant but not overly ornate, soft painterly detail, dramatic but readable lighting, no text, no logo, no watermark.

Full body character reference of Selya, a 17-year-old elven girl, calm and ethereal. She has long silver-white hair reaching her waist with a slight blue sheen under light, soft right-parted bangs revealing her left eye, pale amber eyes that look clear and gentle, an oval teenage face, delicate but not glamorous. Her expression is quiet, restrained, with hidden sadness, as if she knows she may disappear someday.

She wears a layered white robe: translucent white outer gauze, soft ivory inner fabric, pale gold trim around the collar, a deep blue waist sash, and a simple deep blue hair ribbon tying her hair loosely at the back. A plain silver bracelet on her right wrist, never removed.

Subtle sealing markings appear on her left hand and forearm: warm golden light traces intertwined with dark violet cracks, shaped like ice fractures and thin tree branches, elegant and tragic, not monstrous. Her hands rest naturally in front of her body, posture quiet and light.

Light neutral background, full body from head to toe, front view with slight three-quarter softness, high quality anime fantasy character concept art.

```

## 验收标准

- 第一眼：安静、干净、有“会消失”的感觉。
- 第二眼：能记住银白长发、浅琥珀眼、深蓝发绳、白袍、银手镯。
- 封印纹路要像冰裂/树枝，不要像怪物感染。
- 她不能看起来很媚，也不能像小孩。
- 角色设定图可以很简单，但气质必须成立。
