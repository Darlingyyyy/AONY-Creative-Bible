# AONY 黄皮纸复古人物提示词 v1

> 用途：用于生成 AONY 核心人物的“旧王国档案 / 冒险者手札 / 黄皮纸人物图鉴”风格图。  
> 目标：避开常见现代二游立绘感，建立更有历史感、档案感、命运感的视觉体系。

## 统一风格说明

所有人物图都建议使用同一种视觉语言：

- aged yellow parchment paper
- vintage fantasy character archive
- hand-drawn ink linework
- muted watercolor wash
- old adventurer notebook illustration
- ancient manuscript character plate
- soft sepia tone
- faded pigments
- no readable text
- no logo
- no watermark

## 通用中文指令模板

每次跑图时，可以先复制这一段，再接具体角色提示词：

```text
请生成一张黄皮纸复古风格的幻想人物图鉴插画。

画面要求：
成年幻想角色，全身，从头到脚完整出现。
背景是旧黄皮纸、羊皮纸、古旧档案纸质感。
不要生成任何可读文字，不要 logo，不要水印。
整体像古代冒险者手札、旧王国档案、人物图鉴中的记录页。
风格是手绘墨线 + 低饱和水彩 + 轻微褪色纸张质感。
不要现代二游立绘感，不要现代 glossy digital look。

请保留人物轮廓清楚、道具清楚、表情克制、气质有故事感。
```

# 01 塞莉亚 Selya

## 角色方向

**森林修道院长大的封印书记官。**  
她不是圣女，不是公主，不是精灵女神，而像古老图书馆里负责抄写封印文献的人。

## 核心识别

- 银白长发
- 浅琥珀眼
- 深蓝旧布发绳
- 银手镯
- 封印纹路
- 旧书、羽毛笔、封蜡、小铜钥匙
- 安静、命运感、像一本快要散页的旧书

角色描述：
Selya is an adult elven woman designed as a seal archivist raised in an old forest monastery. She has long silver-white hair reaching her waist, slightly uneven at the tips, with a few thin strands bound by tiny thread loops and small aged copper rings. Her hair is tied loosely at the back with a simple deep blue cloth ribbon, old and slightly worn rather than decorative.

Her eyes are pale amber, quiet and memorable. Her expression is calm, restrained, and inward-looking, as if she has read too many prophecies and accepted one of them as her own.

She wears an ivory inner robe with simple flowing shapes, over it a short worn gray-blue shoulder cape, like something used in an old library or forest archive. The cuffs and hem have tiny hand-drawn rune borders, subtle and faded. Around her waist hangs a small leather booklet, a sealed wax token, a broken feather pen, and a tiny brass key.

On one wrist she wears a plain silver bracelet with a single fine crack. On one hand and forearm, there are delicate sealing patterns like pale gold ink and muted violet-gray lines, shaped like ancient writing mixed with branch patterns. The markings look symbolic, elegant, and archival, not like battle magic.

Composition:
Full body centered on aged yellow parchment paper. Around her, include small hand-drawn artifact sketches without readable text: a deep blue cloth ribbon, a cracked silver bracelet, a closed old book, a broken feather pen, and a small branching seal pattern.
